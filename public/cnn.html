  1. 1 · draw sketch a digit below, or load a real MNIST test digit — it gets centered and scaled the way MNIST digits are, then handed to the network as 28×28 numbers.
  2. 2 · kernels slide eight learned 3×3 kernels slide across the image, and each one leaves behind a feature map — a picture of where that little pattern was found.
  3. 3 · relu, then pooling relu throws away the negative responses and keeps the positive evidence; max pooling then keeps only the strongest value in each 2×2 block, shrinking the map but holding on to what mattered.
  4. 4 · the dense layer votes the last maps flatten into 400 numbers and one dense layer votes on the ten digits. Curious how a single kernel gets its map? Scan mode below lets you watch one kernel do its work, one pixel at a time.
