  1. 1 · tokenize your text becomes tokens — one per character, drawn from the 65 characters this model ever saw. Anything outside that alphabet is dropped.
  2. 2 · embed each token becomes 64 numbers, and its position in the window contributes 64 more; the two are added, so the same letter means something different early and late.
  3. 3 · attend four heads per layer look back along the context and pull in what they find — the arcs and the triangle below are those real weights.
  4. 4 · sample the output scores become a weighted dice roll, shaped by temperature, top-k and top-p. The sampled character appends to the text and the whole thing runs again.

generate

order: temperature reshapes the scores, then top-k keeps the k best, then top-p keeps the smallest set of those already worth p. What survives is renormalized — that is what the bars show and what gets sampled.

greedy always takes the largest score, which none of the three can change — so they switch off while it is on.
